Outreach principles

1. Alumni first

Shared school / course creates a natural reason to contact.

2. Reachable seniority

Analyst, Associate and VP are usually better first contacts than MD-level outreach.

3. One thoughtful reason

Reference a team, product, deal, background or investment approach. Avoid generic praise.

4. Short ask

Ask for a brief conversation or one specific insight; do not send an essay.

5. Track the follow-up

Record replies, conversations and the next action like a startup CRM.
